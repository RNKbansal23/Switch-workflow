# Research sources, coverage, and unresolved details

[Back to index](../../README.md)

Checked **November 4, 2026** using the public web reader. Notes summarize public text and link to the authoritative pages; they are not a copy of the sites.

## What was explored

The hackathon's Challenge, Prizes, Tracks, Judging, FAQ, Overview, and contribution-assignment links were followed. They resolve to sections of the same overview page. Resources, Schedule, and Rules resolve to separate pages and were also opened. The research followed event links into Switch documentation, GitHub, onboarding, Wispr, and the community invite.

This was a text-based navigation review, not an interactive browser session. It did **not** click every JavaScript control or inspect every state of every accordion. The registration area exposed only a loading message. Cookie preferences, feedback controls, account flows, video playback, and social actions were not exercised. No registration, submission, repository star, issue comment, installation, or other account action was performed.

Coverage concentrates on the hackathon and Switch Rooms documentation. The documentation index also includes separate Switch Trust, ROI, SDK, and legal product families; their entire documentation was not exhaustively reviewed. The support guardrail example was reviewed where it relates to Rooms.
