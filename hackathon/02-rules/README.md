# Official rules: participant summary

[Back to index](../../README.md)

Source: [Official rules, sections 1–8](https://www.wemakedevs.org/hackathons/switch/rules), checked November 4, 2026. This summary does not replace those terms.

- Participants must be at least 18, or their jurisdiction's higher age of majority.
- Geographic eligibility remains subject to sanctions, export controls, and local restrictions; the sponsor may change exclusions.
- Enter personally using one accurate, verifiable identity. Automated registration/submission, multiple identities, temporary email accounts, and participation manipulation are prohibited. AI-assisted development is separately permitted.
- Sponsor personnel, judges, specified relatives, and household members cannot win. Employment or institutional restrictions may also apply.
- You retain ownership, but grant the sponsor a broad, irrevocable license for event operation, evaluation, documentation, and promotion.
- Obtain necessary rights, respect third-party licenses, and exclude malicious material and others' confidential information.
- Violations, misleading information, failed verification, tampering, or disruptive conduct can cause disqualification or prize recovery.
- Winners handle taxes and requested verification/documentation. Prizes normally cannot be transferred; the sponsor can substitute. Unclaimed prizes may lapse after 30 days.
- The sponsor can change, suspend, or cancel the event. Its decisions are final.

Also review the [WeMakeDevs code of conduct](https://www.wemakedevs.org/coc). Technical participation requirements are in [overview](../01-overview/README.md); contribution-specific requirements are in [core contributions](../10-core-contributions/README.md).
